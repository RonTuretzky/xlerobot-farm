What we have decided

TopicCurrent directionStatus
WateringA small robot-held pouring bottle. Human refill.Chosen; grip/spout and accurate pours untested.
GrowingRectangular wicking planter and square covered germination kit. Each can finish a crop.Prints in progress; leak/wick tests pending.
SensingOne removable light paddle. Moisture probe and relay deferred for paper trays.Recommended first build; human water/wetness checks before refills.
CamerasUse the robot’s head/wrist cameras initially.No separate overhead camera in the first build.
ComputerThe Mac connects to robot interfaces, cameras and ESP32 through the owned powered USB hub.Physical connections still to commission.
FurnitureFree fully printed modular table for the planters; separate cart for the robot.Table chosen; editable model files still missing.
SoftwarePersistent care records, validity checks, restart recovery, exception viewer and replay.Roadmap only; not implemented by this website.

The next three useful actions

  1. Finish the small planter parts, leak-test and sow cress. Seeds added to the existing cart; purchase is still yours to complete.
  2. Download the table’s model files. The ZIP received contains Prusa MINI G-code, not the 13 editable 3MF models.
  3. Build the simulated evidence/recovery loop before travel. Physical robot commissioning remains September 30.
Removed from V0: electric watering pump, external reservoir, hoses/float switches, scale hardware, separate overhead camera and leak pads. The requested silicone mat, adhesive rubber sheets, plant labels and measuring syringes remain out. Use household measuring materials.
